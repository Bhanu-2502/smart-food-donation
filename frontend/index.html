How It Works

🍱

Donate

Donors post details about their surplus food and pickup location.

📍

Connect

FoodConnect helps identify nearby NGOs that can collect the food.

🤝

Collect

NGOs accept the donation and arrange collection from the donor.

❤️

Make an Impact

Surplus food reaches people who need it instead of becoming waste.

Small Actions Can Make a Big Difference

Every meal donated is one less meal wasted. FoodConnect brings donors and NGOs together to make food redistribution easier, faster and more meaningful.
